OLIVE — POLICY

Please take a moment to review the booking policies before submitting an inquiry.

Booking

Please allow at least one week’s notice whenever possible before booking a portrait session or event. All bookings begin with an inquiry. Availability is offered on a first-come, first-served basis.

Payment & Retainer

A 50% retainer is required to reserve your date. This amount is applied toward your total balance. The remaining balance is due on the day of the session or before final delivery. Accepted payment methods include Zelle and Venmo.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Cancellations made at least 24 hours in advance are eligible for a full retainer refund or can be transferred to a rescheduled session. If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, the retainer is non-refundable, and a new booking and deposit will be required for a future session.

Photo Delivery

Clients receive their edited, high-resolution images via a private virtual gallery within 3 to 7 days. You will receive the final images included in your selected collection. Additional edited photos are available for $20 USD each. Raw or unedited files are not delivered.

Usage & Rights

For personal sessions (portraits, events, etc.), you have full freedom to use, print, and share your photos freely once paid in full. For commercial projects, brand campaigns, or business advertising, additional licensing terms and agreements apply.

Questions

If you have any questions regarding booking, services, or payments, please reach out before submitting your inquiry.
